Join us

Work with us

CVSP studies computer vision, multimodal AI, 3D/4D spatial intelligence, and robot perception. We are looking for undergraduate interns, graduate students, and postdoctoral researchers.

Undergraduate

Research intern

Email a short note about the computer vision topics you want to work on. Programming and basic math help, but they are not required to start.

Graduate

M.S. / Ph.D. student

Contact the lab before applying to the Graduate School of Electrical & Electronics Engineering. Send your transcript, CV, and research interests. After a preliminary interview with the professor, there is a department interview and a lab interview. Interning with us first is strongly recommended.

Researcher

Researcher / Postdoc

Send your transcript, CV, and research interests. The process is similar to the graduate track and starts with a preliminary interview.

What we value

  • A respectful culture where everyone's ideas are heard
  • Collaboration across projects and with industry partners
  • Self-motivated students who own their research
  • Flexible working hours and locations
